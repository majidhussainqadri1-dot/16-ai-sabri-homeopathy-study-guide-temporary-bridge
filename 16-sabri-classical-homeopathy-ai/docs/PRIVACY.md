# Privacy

AI sessions, questions, answers, citations, feedback, usage counts and retention dates are stored to provide the requested service. External providers receive only approved source excerpts and minimized/redacted prompts when explicitly configured.

The default corpus excludes private clinical records, private messages, identity evidence and private File 15 studies. Provider training is disabled by policy and must be contractually verified before external-provider activation.

Users can export or erase account-owned sessions through plugin endpoints and WordPress privacy tools. Audit records never intentionally store raw prompts or secrets.
