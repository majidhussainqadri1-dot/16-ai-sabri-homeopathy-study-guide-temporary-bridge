# Operations

Kill switches: global AI disabled, AI Teacher disabled, external provider unavailable/fallback. Monitor provider availability, approved source count/freshness, request/refusal/citation/output failures, cost budgets, rate limits, outbox lag/dead letters, teacher queue age/failures and retention completion.

AI Teacher target: four configured schedule slots, not four guaranteed public posts; safety, source, budget, human review and canonical owner availability may correctly prevent publication. Operators must never bypass these gates merely to hit a count.

Incident flow: contain/disable, preserve scrubbed evidence, notify File 24, retract affected source/draft through owners, replay idempotently after correction, conduct post-incident review and Founder ratification for permanent changes.
